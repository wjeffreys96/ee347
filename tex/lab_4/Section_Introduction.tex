\paragraph{}The purpose of this lab is to develop a familiarization with transformer usage in two common situations.
First we will create a three phase transformer bank in a delta-wye configuration. With the bank energized we will 
then collect the bank and phase (or primary and secondary) voltage, current and power in order to compare them with calculated 
values. This first type of transformer bank is common on industrial sites using heavy equipment and large three phase motors.
The second type of transformer bank is for residential use cases and uses only two phases. This is the familiar
120/208V that we use to run household appliances from the toaster to the dryer. In building these circuits and performing these
we will be enhancing our understanding with hands on experience. Understanding how to design these two transformer circuits will
be crucial in a career in power engineering because of how common they are, and is something every electrical engineer should be
comfortable with.
