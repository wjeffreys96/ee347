\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{ The lockout/tagout panel shall be used.}\\
    \textbf{The lockout relay was wired up and is required to be used
    to operate the panel.}

  \item \emph{ Power shall be provided to the control circuits via
    appropriate PLC outputs.}\\
    \textbf{The power to the control circuits is provided via PLC
    24VDC outputs.}

  \item \emph{ The PLC shall be powered by a 120 VAC source.}\\
    \textbf{The PLC is powered by a 120 VAC source.}

  \item \emph{ The design shall be implemented using a CLICK PLC.}\\
    \textbf{The design is implemented using a CLICK PLC.}

  \item \emph{ The design shall be implemented using the CLICK
    Programming Software.}\\
    \textbf{The design is implemented using the CLICK Programming Software.}

  \item \emph{ Power to the PLC shall be protected by a
    lock-out/tag-out mechanism.}\\
    \textbf{Power to the PLC is protected by a lock-out/tag-out mechanism.}

  \item \emph{ The PLC shall be protected on the 120 VAC side by an
    appropriately-rated fuse.}\\
    \textbf{
      The PLC is protected on the 120 VAC side by a 1A fuse. This
      decision is supported by
      a maximum current rating of 0.982A and the NFPA 240.6
    }

  \item \emph{ All components shall be mounted on a panel.}\\
    \textbf{All components are mounted on a panel.}

  \item \emph{ Strain reliefs or rubber grommets shall be used for
    all panel thru-holes.}\\
    \textbf{Rubber grommets are used on all panel thru-holes.}

  \item \emph{ Appropriate and consistent color coding shall be used
    for all conductors and terminal blocks.}\\
    \textbf{Appropriate and consistent color coding is used for all
      conductors and terminal blocks.
    Line is red, neutral is white, green is ground, and black is 0v.}
  \item \emph{ The following items shall be mounted on the panel:
      \begin{itemize}
        \item A green 22 mm pilot
        \item A red 22 mm pilot
        \item A blue 22 mm momentary button
    \end{itemize}}
    \textbf{A green 22mm pilot, a red 22 mm pilot, and a blue
    momentary button are all mounted on the panel.}

  \item \emph{ The green pilot light shall turn on when the circuit
    is first energized.}\\
    \textbf{The green pilot light turns on when the circuit is first energized.}

  \item \emph{ The red pilot light shall turn on when the blue
    momentary button is depressed if the green pilot light is on.}\\
    \textbf{When the momentary button is pushed, if the green light
    is on, the green light turns off, and the red light turns on.}

  \item \emph{ The red pilot shall remain on after the blue momentary
    button is released.}\\
    \textbf{The red pilot light remains on after the blue momentary
    button is released.}

  \item \emph{ The green pilot light shall turn on when the blue
    momentary button is depressed if the red pilot light is on.}\\
    \textbf{If the red pilot light is on when the blue momentary
      button is pressed, the green pilot light turns on and the red
    light turns off.}

  \item \emph{ The green pilot shall remain on after the blue
    momentary button is released.}\\
    \textbf{The green pilot remains on after the blue momentary
    button is released.}
\end{enumerate}
