\begin{enumerate}
\item Plug in the power cord
\item Remove the lock out locks on the lockout relay
\item Press the button at the top of the rack to turn power on to the circuit
\item Flip the switch on the CLICK PLC to the Run position
\item Observe the green light is illuminated
\item Press the blue button
\item Observe the red light is illuminated and the green light is no longer illuminated
\item Turn off the power to the rack
\item Replace the locks on the lockout relay
\end{enumerate}
