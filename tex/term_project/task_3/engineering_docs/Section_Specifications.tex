\begin{enumerate}
	\item \emph{ The lockout/tagout panel shall be used.}\\
	\textbf{The lockout relay was wired up and is required to be used
		to operate the panel.}
	
	\item \emph{ Power shall be provided to the control circuits via
		appropriate \gls{plc} outputs.}\\
	\textbf{The power to the control circuits is provided via \gls{plc}
		24VDC outputs.}
	
	\item \emph{ The \gls{plc} shall be powered by a 120 VAC source.}\\
	\textbf{The \gls{plc} is powered by a 120 VAC source.}
	
	\item \emph{ The design shall be implemented using a CLICK \gls{plc}.}\\
	\textbf{The design is implemented using a CLICK \gls{plc}.}
	
	\item \emph{ The design shall be implemented using the CLICK
		Programming Software.}\\
	\textbf{The design is implemented using the CLICK Programming Software.}
	
	\item \emph{ Power to the \gls{plc} shall be protected by a
		lock-out/tag-out mechanism.}\\
	\textbf{Power to the \gls{plc} is protected by a lock-out/tag-out mechanism.}
	
	\item \emph{ The \gls{plc} shall be protected on the 120 VAC side by an
		appropriately-rated fuse.}\\
	\textbf{
		The \gls{plc} is protected on the 120 VAC side by a 1A fuse. This
		decision is supported by
		a maximum current rating of 0.982A and \gls{nfpa} 70 (\gls{nec}) 240.6.
	}
	
	\item \emph{ All components shall be mounted on a panel.}\\
	\textbf{All components are mounted on a panel.}
	
	\item \emph{ Strain reliefs or rubber grommets shall be used for
		all panel thru-holes.}\\
	\textbf{Rubber grommets are used on all panel thru-holes.}
	
	\item \emph{ Appropriate and consistent color coding shall be used
		for all conductors and terminal blocks.}\\
	\textbf{Appropriate and consistent color coding is used for all
		conductors and terminal blocks.
		Line is red, neutral is white, green is ground, and black is 0v.}
	
	\item \emph{ A red light shall be illuminated when the main
		intertie point is energized.}\\
	\textbf{A red light is illuminated when the main intertie point
		is energized.}
	
	\item \emph{ Each switchgear within the substation shall be
		associated with the following:
		\begin{itemize}
			\item One 22 mm red pilot light
			\item One 22 mm green pilot light
			\item One 22 mm blue button
			\item One three-phase contactor
	\end{itemize}}
	\textbf{Each switchgear within the substation is associated with
		one 22 mm red pilot light,
		one 22 mm green pilot light, one 22 mm blue button, and one
		three-phase contactor.}
	
	\item \emph{ Each three-phase contactor shall be sized to handle
		the rated system voltage and the full-load current demanded by
		downstream assets.}\\
	\textbf{Each three-phase contactor is sized to handle the rated
		system voltage of [VOLTAGE] V
		and the full-load current demanded by its downstream assets.
		The selected contactors are rated
		for [CONTACTOR CURRENT RATING] A, which exceeds the calculated
		full-load current of [FLA] A.}
	
	\item \emph{ When switchgear is open, the green light associated
		with the switchgear shall be illuminated.}\\
	\textbf{When switchgear is open, the green light associated with
		the switchgear is illuminated.}
	
	\item \emph{ When switchgear is open, the red light associated with
		the switchgear shall be off.}\\
	\textbf{When switchgear is open, the red light associated with
		the switchgear is off.}
	
	\item \emph{ When switchgear is closed, the red light associated
		with the switchgear shall be illuminated.}\\
	\textbf{When switchgear is closed, the red light associated with
		the switchgear is illuminated.}
	
	\item \emph{ When switchgear is closed, the green light associated
		with the switchgear shall be off.}\\
	\textbf{When switchgear is closed, the green light associated
		with the switchgear is off.}
	
	\item \emph{ The status of all switchgear shall be determined using
		auxiliary contacts that are mechanically ganged to the power contactors.}\\
	\textbf{The status of all switchgear is determined using
		auxiliary contacts that are mechanically
		ganged to the power contactors. These contacts are wired to \gls{plc} inputs.}
	
	\item \emph{ Each switchgear within the substation shall be
		controlled using one blue 22 mm button.}\\
	\textbf{Each switchgear within the substation is controlled using
		one blue 22 mm button.}
	
	\item \emph{ Each button shall be labeled to indicate its
		associated switchgear.}\\
	\textbf{Each button is labeled to indicate its associated switchgear.}
	
	\item \emph{ Each switchgear shall energize when its associated
		blue button is depressed if the switchgear is de-energized.}\\
	\textbf{When the blue button is depressed and its associated
		switchgear is de-energized,
		the switchgear energizes.}
	
	\item \emph{ Each switchgear shall de-energize when its associated
		blue momentary button is depressed if the switchgear is energized.}\\
	\textbf{When the blue momentary button is depressed and its
		associated switchgear is energized,
		the switchgear de-energizes.}
	
	\item \emph{ The bus switchgear and all feeder switchgear shall be
		open if the transformer switchgear is open. These switchgear may
		be open or closed if the transformer switchgear is closed.}\\
	\textbf{If the transformer switchgear is open, the bus switchgear
		and all feeder switchgear are held
		open. If the transformer switchgear is closed, the bus
		switchgear and feeder switchgear
		may be open or closed.}
	
	\item \emph{ All feeder switchgear shall be open if the bus
		switchgear is open. These switchgear may be open or closed if the
		bus switchgear is closed.}\\
	\textbf{If the bus switchgear is open, all feeder switchgear are
		held open. If the bus switchgear
		is closed, the feeder switchgear may be open or closed.}
	
	\item \emph{ The motor load should have properly-sized over-current
		protection.}\\
	\textbf{The motor load is protected by a [OVERLOAD RELAY /
		BREAKER TYPE] rated for [RATING] A.
		This decision is supported by a motor full-load current of [FLA]
		A and \gls{nfpa} 70 (\gls{nec}) Article 430.}
	
	\item \emph{ If the motor overloads, the control system should
		indicate the fault.}\\
	\textbf{If the motor overloads, the control system indicates the
		fault via [FAULT INDICATOR, e.g., a dedicated pilot light or
		\gls{hmi} indication].}
	
	\item \emph{ If the motor overloads, the control system should open
		the associated feeder switchgear (transfer trip) and not affect
		the state of any other switchgear.}\\
	\textbf{If the motor overloads, the control system opens the
		associated feeder switchgear
		(transfer trip). The state of all other switchgear is not affected.}
	
	\item \emph{ The motor speed may be controllable via a
		\gls{vfd}.}\\
	\textbf{The motor speed is controllable via a [VFD MODEL]
		\gls{vfd}.
		[OR: A \gls{vfd} was not implemented in this design.]}
\end{enumerate}
